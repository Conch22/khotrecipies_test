# [Chettinad Chicken Chukka]()

Chettinad Chicken Chukka with bold, roasted flavours!

### Ingredients

- [ ] 8 Kashmiri red chillies
- [ ] 1 tbsp coriander seeds
- [ ] 1 tbsp fennel seeds
- [ ] 1 inch cinnamon
- [ ] 4 green cardamoms
- [ ] 1 sprig curry leaves
- [ ] 2 tbsp cashew nuts
- [ ] 2 tbsp roasted chana dal
- [ ] 1/2 cup fresh grated coconut
- [ ] 1/4 cup sesame oil
- [ ] 1 sprig curry leaves
- [ ] 1/2 cup shallots
- [ ] 1 tbsp ginger paste
- [ ] 1 tbsp garlic paste
- [ ] 1 kg chicken
- [ ] 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
- [ ] 1 tbsp sesame oil
- [ ] salt, to taste
- [ ] 1/2 cup water
- [ ] 1/2 lemon lemon juice, juice of
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

### Instructions

#### For the spice mix
- Heat a kadhai and dry roast Kashmiri red chillies, coriander seeds, fennel seeds, cinnamon, green cardamoms and curry leaves.
- Add cashew nuts and roasted chana dal and roast again. Finally add fresh grated coconut and roast till it turns golden brown.
- Cool completely and grind into a fine powder.
#### For the chicken
- Heat sesame oil in a kadhai. Add curry leaves and shallots.
- Sauté till the shallots turn golden brown.
- Add ginger paste and garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
- Add the chicken and sauté on a high flame for 5 minutes.
- Add turmeric powder, the prepared spice powder and sesame oil. Mix well on a medium flame.
- Add salt and water. Cover and cook till the chicken is done and the oil starts separating.
- Once the oil separates, remove the lid and continue sautéing till all the moisture evaporates and the masala coats the chicken beautifully.
- Finish with lemon juice and fresh coriander. Mix once and serve hot.
