# [Dal Khichdi](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nBvlmriTucQ)

### Ingredients

- [ ] 1 cup basmati rice
- [ ] salt
- [ ] 1 cup toor dal
- [ ] 1/4 cup chana dal
- [ ] 1/4 cup masoor dal
- [ ] 1 tsp ghee
- [ ] 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
- [ ] 1 inch ginger, julienned
- [ ] 5 cups water
- [ ] 1 tsp turmeric
- [ ] 1 tbsp ghee
- [ ] 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
- [ ] 2 onion, medium, chopped
- [ ] 2-3 whole red chillies
- [ ] 2 tbsp garlic, chopped
- [ ] 3-4 green chillies, chopped
- [ ] 1 inch ginger, chopped
- [ ] 2 tomatoes, medium, chopped
- [ ] 1 tsp turmeric powder
- [ ] 1 tbsp coriander powder
- [ ] 1 tsp red chilli powder
- [ ] 1 tsp amchur powder
- [ ] 1 tsp garam masala
- [ ] 1 tsp kasuri methi
- [ ] 1 live charcoal
- [ ] 1 tsp kasuri methi
- [ ] 1 tsp lemon juice
- [ ] 1 tbsp fresh coriander leaves, chopped
- [ ] 1 tbsp ghee
- [ ] 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
- [ ] 1 tsp red chilli powder
- [ ] 1/2 tsp asafoetida (hing)
- [ ] 2-3 whole red chillies
- [ ] 3 tbsp fried onions

### Instructions

#### Rice
- Rinse basmati rice thoroughly with water. Soak for 10-15 minutes.
- Add the soaked rice and salt to taste to boiling water in a pot and cook for 10-15 minutes. Strain the cooked rice and set aside.
#### Dal
- Wash toor dal, chana dal and masoor dal thoroughly. Soak for 30 minutes, then drain.
- Heat ghee in a pressure cooker. Add cumin seeds (jeera) and let them splutter. Add ginger, the soaked and drained dal, water, turmeric and salt to taste.
- Stir and pressure cook on medium flame for 3-4 whistles. Switch off the flame and let the cooker depressurize naturally before opening the lid. Give it a nice stir.
#### Masala
- Heat ghee in a wok. Add cumin seeds (jeera) and let them splutter.
- Add onion and cook until translucent, about 5 minutes.
- Add whole red chillies, garlic, green chillies and ginger. Sauté for 1 minute.
- Add tomatoes, salt to taste, turmeric powder, coriander powder, red chilli powder, amchur powder, garam masala and kasuri methi. Cook until the tomatoes are mushy.
#### Combine
- Add the cooked dal and mix well.
- Add the cooked rice, mix well and cook for 3-4 minutes.
- Add water to adjust the consistency and cook for another 4-5 minutes.
#### Smoke
- Place a live charcoal in a small bowl set on the khichdi and pour a few drops of ghee. Cover and smoke for 3-4 minutes.
#### Finish
- Remove the charcoal. Add kasuri methi and lemon juice and give it a nice stir. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
#### Tadka
- Heat ghee in a pan. Add cumin seeds (jeera), red chilli powder, asafoetida (hing), whole red chillies and fried onions. Mix, then pour over the hot khichdi and serve.
